SOW §14: "The Vendor shall maintain a risk register and escalation matrix, updated monthly and shared
with TMC IT." The escalation matrix is in [RACI and Team §4](raci-team-structure.md#4-escalation-matrix-project-phase).

## 1. Method

- **Probability (P)** and **Impact (I)** are scored 1 (low) to 5 (very high); **Rating = P × I**:
  1–6 Low, 8–12 Medium, 15–25 High.
- Every risk has one owner. High risks are reported to the Project Steering Committee every month.
- Risks are reviewed at each fortnightly project review; closed risks remain in the register with the
  closure date.
