## Part A: Queries

| No. | Document and clause | Observation | Query / clarification requested |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q-01 | SOW §7, first paragraph; milestone table | §7 states "The indicative total project duration is 150 days from the date of Contract Award to Project Closure", whereas the milestone table places M5 at T + 180 days and M6 (Project Closure) at T + 210 days. | Kindly confirm which duration governs. We request that the milestone table (T + 210 days) be treated as the indicative schedule. |
| Q-02 | SOW §8.1, fourth bullet | §8.1 lists "The TMC website and four unit websites, developed, tested, and deployed", whereas §4.1, §11.2 and the Invitation refer to five constituent unit websites (six in total). | Kindly confirm that the deliverable is the TMC website and **five** unit websites. |
| Q-03 | SOW §11.2, illustration of Financial Score | For Bidder A, A = 120 and B = 20 are shown with E = 270; A + B = 140. With E(low) = 140, the F-scores of Bidders B and C would be 38.9 and 44.4 rather than 75 and 85.7. | Kindly issue a corrected illustration so that bidders apply the formula consistently. |
| Q-04 | Invitation, "Mode of submission: E-Tender Single Packet" and closing note 4; SOW §12, §12.2, §12.3 | The Invitation specifies a single packet and states that the tender will be published on the GeM portal after finalisation of the scope; the SOW requires a Technical Bid and a Financial Bid in two parts, with no cost element in the Technical Bid, and refers the EMD and performance security to the EOI notice (which states EMD "NIL" and does not specify performance security). | Kindly confirm that (a) at this EOI stage only the documents listed in the Invitation are to be submitted, **without any financial information**; (b) the subsequent tender will follow the two-part submission of SOW §12 and the portal on which it will be published; and (c) the EMD and performance security applicable at tender stage. |
| Q-05 | Invitation title and EOI checklist ("Website Design & Development Service", "supply, installation and commissioning"); SOW §1, §3 | The SOW states that "Design work is not within the scope of this EOI; the successful bidder shall implement the design as provided" and that no re-design proposal or design fee shall be entertained. | Kindly confirm that the scope excludes design work and that the tender title may be aligned to "Development, CMS Implementation, Deployment, Content Migration, Security Certification, Training, Warranty and Maintenance of the TMC Website Ecosystem". |
| Q-06 | SOW §3 and Annexures table (p. 20) | Annexures A (design system), B (IA and page-to-template mapping, also the page list), C (Figma) and D (indicative BoQ and financial bid format) are listed as "Will be provided by TMC with this EOI document", but were not part of the published documents; §3 also states they "shall be shared … at the time of the EOI, if deemed necessary by TMC". | Kindly share Annexures A–C (under a non-disclosure undertaking if required), or at least the number of page templates, components and content types per website, so that effort can be estimated; and kindly confirm whether Annexure D will be shared at the EOI meeting. |
| Q-07 | SOW §4.7 (seventh bullet), §4.10 (second bullet), §4.14, §7.1 | Performance thresholds and peak traffic are to be "agreed with TMC" but are not stated. | Kindly indicate the target metrics (for example Lighthouse performance score, Largest Contentful Paint, Cumulative Layout Shift per template, desktop and mobile), the expected peak concurrent users or requests per second, and current traffic statistics of the existing websites. |
| Q-08 | SOW §6.4 (availability 99.5 %) read with §4.7 (hosting on TMC-owned or TMC-subscribed infrastructure) | The Vendor does not control the availability of TMC-owned infrastructure, network or cloud subscription. | Kindly confirm that outages attributable to TMC-owned infrastructure, network, power, DNS or cloud provider, outside the Vendor's control, are excluded from the availability computation, and the method of measuring availability. |
| Q-09 | SOW §4.4 ("wherever applicable"), §4.12 | The number and nature of TMC-developed applications requiring front ends (online forms, patient services, EMR, appointments, payments, examinations and results) are not listed. | Kindly provide the list of applications and screens in scope, their API specifications and authentication methods, and whether test endpoints will be available during development. |
| Q-10 | SOW §4.11 | The volume of content to be migrated is not indicated. | Kindly indicate, per website, the approximate number of pages, documents (PDF) and images; the source systems and formats in which content will be provided; whether Hindi content is provided for all pages; and whether an inventory of existing URLs will be provided for redirect mapping. |
| Q-11 | SOW §4.8, §7 (M4, M6), §7 paragraph on delays, §7.1 | STQC certification and Safe-to-Host depend on external bodies whose lead times are outside the Vendor's control; STQC is due by M6 (T + 210). | Kindly confirm that delays attributable to certifying bodies will be treated as "reasons beyond the Vendor's reasonable control" under §7, and that §7.1 ("STQC certification obtained or under process, as applicable to the milestone") applies to M4 and M5. |
| Q-12 | SOW §6.4 | "Business hours" and "business day" are not defined; the SLA has no Low priority and no response times. | Kindly define business hours (for example 09:00–18:00 IST, Monday to Saturday, excluding Government of India holidays at Mumbai), and indicate whether Low-priority resolution and response times are to be proposed by the bidder. |
| Q-13 | Invitation, first paragraph and Eligibility Criteria (i)–(ii); SOW §9 | The Invitation invites "Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEM), Software development vendor or Authorized Agent/Distributor", asks for experience of "supply, installation and commissioning" and a local office and service engineer "for attending the breakdown calls"; SOW §9 sets detailed criteria for a registered software firm. | Kindly confirm that the eligibility criteria of SOW §9 govern, and whether "local office and service support" requires an office in Mumbai or whether an office elsewhere in India with on-site support as required is acceptable. |
| Q-14 | Invitation, "Objective" paragraph | The paragraph refers to "clinical requirement to serve patient care" and to reducing "turnover time in diagnostic and treatment to cancer patients", which appears to be carried over from an equipment EOI. | Kindly confirm that the objective of this EOI is as stated in SOW §2. |
| Q-15 | SOW §4.10 (third bullet) read with §4.7 (data residency) | The analytics and search console tooling are "TMC-approved" but not named; §4.7 requires that all data, including logs, reside in India. | Kindly indicate the approved tool(s). We suggest a self-hosted, open-source analytics tool on TMC infrastructure in India (see S-02). |
| Q-16 | SOW table of contents ("11.2 Financial Evaluation — 30 Marks") and §11 | §11 assigns a 30 % weightage to the financial bid and computes F out of 100. | Kindly confirm that "30 Marks" in the table of contents denotes the 30 % weightage. |
| Q-17 | SOW §4.7 (Disaster Recovery, RPO 15 minutes, RTO one hour, periodic drills) | The DR location, the provider of DR infrastructure and the drill frequency are not specified. | Kindly indicate whether TMC will provide the DR infrastructure (and in which city/region in India), and the expected frequency of DR drills (we propose quarterly). |
| Q-18 | SOW §4.13 | Additional languages "up to a total of three to four" may be added later. | Kindly indicate the languages anticipated, so that fonts and scripts can be provided for in the design system. |
| Q-19 | SOW §6.1 read with §7 (M5, M6) | §6.1 defines Project Closure as "Go-Live of all websites in scope" (M5), whereas the milestone table places Project Closure at M6 (certification, handover). | Kindly confirm the date from which the 12-month warranty commences. |
| Q-20 | SOW §4.12 (third bullet), §4.4 | The payment gateway for donations is "TMC-approved" but not named. | Kindly indicate the payment gateway, the integration method (redirect / hosted page), availability of sandbox credentials, and confirm that receipts are generated by TMC's systems. We understand that no card or bank data will be handled or stored by the website. |
| Q-21 | SOW §4.1; Invitation introduction (list of TMC centres) | §4.1 lists "HBCH, New Chandigarh" (hbchpunjab.tmc.gov.in) and "MPMMCC / HBCH, Varanasi" (one website); the Invitation's introduction lists HBCH Sangrur and HBCH & RC Mullanpur (Punjab), and HBCH Varanasi and MPMMCC Varanasi separately, together with ACTREC, CCE and BBCI. | Kindly confirm (a) which centre(s) the Punjab website covers; (b) that MPMMCC and HBCH Varanasi share one website; (c) that other TMC centres are outside the present scope; and (d) that DNS records and TLS certificates for the subdomains will be provided by TMC. |
| Q-22 | SOW §4.7 (first and third bullets), §7 (M2) | Hosting is on infrastructure "owned or subscribed by TMC", and Production and DR environments are to be provisioned by M2 (T + 30). | Kindly confirm that TMC will procure and pay for the hosting infrastructure (on-premise or MeitY-empanelled cloud in TMC's name) for Production, DR and UAT, with the Vendor provisioning and configuring the software stack; if any hosting cost is expected in the bid, kindly specify the sizing basis. |
| Q-23 | SOW §4.6 (content workflow) | Workflow notifications (review requested, returned, approved) require an outbound e-mail relay. | Kindly confirm that TMC will provide an SMTP relay and sender address for the websites. |
| Q-24 | SOW §8.2 (repository and access transfer), §4.7 (data residency) | The platform for the version-controlled repository and CI/CD is not specified. | Kindly indicate TMC's preferred repository platform (for example a TMC-hosted Git server in India or a TMC-owned organisation on a hosted service). We confirm that the repository will contain no personal data or live credentials. |
| Q-25 | SOW §4.8 (fourth bullet) | Audit logs are to be "retained and made available to TMC for review"; no retention period is stated. | Kindly specify the required retention period for the CMS audit log and for system logs (we propose the contract period online plus TMC-held archives, and at least 180 days for system logs in India in line with the CERT-In Directions of 28 April 2022). |
| Q-26 | SOW §6.2 | "Minor enhancements within the agreed scope" are included in the AMC but not defined. | Kindly define minor enhancements (for example changes of up to five person-days using existing templates and content types) or an annual allowance. |
| Q-27 | SOW §4.16 | The number of trainees and batches is not indicated. | Kindly indicate the approximate number of participants per role (TMC IT, CMS administrators, TMC-level editors, unit-level editors), the preferred mode (online / at TMH Mumbai), and whether unit-level training at unit locations is expected. |
| Q-28 | SOW §4.8 (sixth bullet), §6.4, §7 (M6) | The STQC certification scheme and whether certification is per website or for the ecosystem, and the issuing authority for the Safe-to-Host certificate, are not specified. | Kindly indicate the STQC certification expected (scope and scheme), whether one certificate for the ecosystem or one per website is required, and the authority whose Safe-to-Host certificate TMC requires. |
| Q-29 | SOW §4.7 (UAT environment), §4.8 (VAPT) | The location of the UAT environment during the project and the environment on which VAPT is to be performed are not specified. | Kindly confirm whether TMC will provide the UAT infrastructure or whether a Vendor-hosted UAT with restricted access (no live personal data) is acceptable during development, and whether VAPT is to be performed on UAT or on the Production environment before Go-Live. |
| Q-30 | SOW §4.3 (indicative volumes) | Each website is "approximately 80–100 CMS-managed pages". | Kindly confirm whether the Hindi version of a page is counted separately, and whether items of content types (tenders, events, job openings, doctor profiles) are included in the page count. |

## Part B: Suggestions for the specifications

| No. | Clause | Suggestion | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| S-01 | §4.10, §7.1 | State measurable page-performance thresholds per template type (for example Core Web Vitals "good" thresholds on mobile and desktop) and the peak load for load testing. | Objective, comparable acceptance criteria for all bidders |
| S-02 | §4.10, §4.7 | Specify a self-hosted, open-source web analytics tool on TMC infrastructure in India, with dashboards accessible to TMC. | Consistent with the data-residency requirement and avoids third-party trackers on patient-facing pages |
| S-03 | §4.5, §13 | Require bidders to list, with their licences, every third-party component (CMS, plugins, modules, libraries, fonts) in the technical bid, and exclude components that require a paid licence key or a non-OSI licence. | Makes the "no proprietary or licensing dependency" requirement verifiable at evaluation |
| S-04 | §6.4 | Add a Low priority level and first-response times for each priority, and define "business hours". | Complete, measurable SLA |
| S-05 | §4.7 | Specify the DR drill frequency (for example quarterly, with a timed report of RPO and RTO achieved). | Makes "periodic drills" verifiable |
| S-06 | §11 | Where a technical presentation is called for, consider a live demonstration of the proposed CMS configuration (roles, review workflow, audit trail, multilingual editing and automatic expiry of notices). | Allows the committee to assess the CMS approach directly |
| S-07 | §4.8 | State that the application front ends shall not persist any personal or clinical data on the website platform and that this shall be demonstrated before Go-Live. | Makes the segregation principle of §4.4/§4.8 testable |
